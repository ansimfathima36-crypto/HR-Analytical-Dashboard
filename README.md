# 📊 HR Analytics Team Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel** to analyze and visualize employee workforce data for **5,000 employees**.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of employee demographics, employment types, department distribution, attrition, overtime, education, and employee stability. It uses **PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPIs, slicers, and interactive visualizations** to transform raw HR data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall employee headcount.
* Understand the distribution of full-time, part-time, contract, and intern employees.
* Analyze workforce diversity by gender.
* Compare employee distribution across departments.
* Analyze employee education levels.
* Understand employee attrition patterns.
* Analyze overtime participation.
* Examine employee stability based on years at the company.
* Create an interactive dashboard for HR decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Headcount Growth

Shows employee headcount trends across different years and employment types.

### 2. Workforce Diversity

Displays the gender distribution of employees:

* Male – 51%
* Female – 49%

### 3. Department Analysis

Provides employee distribution across departments such as:

* Finance
* HR
* IT
* Marketing
* Operations
* R&D
* Sales

### 4. Education Analysis

Visualizes employees based on their highest education level:

* Bachelor
* Diploma
* Master
* PhD

### 5. Attrition Analysis

Analyzes employees who stayed with the organization versus employees who left.

### 6. Overtime Analysis

Shows the percentage of employees working overtime compared with those who do not.

### 7. Employee Stability

Analyzes employees according to their years of experience within the company.

### 8. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for filtering HR data by:

* Department
* Job Role
